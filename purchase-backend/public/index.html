What SnapIT does differently.

Whole-system scan

Every drive you own.

Point SnapIT at your phone, your laptop, the NAS in the cupboard, the external drive in the drawer. It reads every image and every video on every one of them and files them together — deduplicated, dated, organised — in a single library on the storage you pick.

Events, not folders

Sorted by story.

Photos already know when they were taken, where, on what camera. SnapIT clusters them into events the way you'd remember them — the weekend away, the kitchen renovation, the birthday — and gives you a browser that reads like a story of the year, not a filesystem tree.

Netflix-style library

Built for the pile.

Ten years of camera roll, one wall of tiles: rows for years, rows for people, rows for places, rows for events. Photos and videos in the same grid. Duplicates folded automatically. Nothing to name, nothing to file by hand.

What's shipped · what's next.

R·02a shipped Local-first catalog scan. Point SnapIT at folders on any drive or NAS mount — the Rust indexer reads every image and video in place, writes a SQLite catalog next to the originals, generates thumbnails, and never touches your files.
R·02a shipped Household profiles + PIN. Up to 5 profiles in a Family Pack, each with its own view of the library. PINs hash with Argon2id (OWASP-current parameters). Nothing about a profile leaves the machine.
R·02a shipped Signed licence file. Ed25519-signed licence pair verified offline against the pubkey baked into every install. Runs even if our purchase server disappears. Never phones home to check validity.
R·02b · in build Signed macOS build. Universal binary (Apple silicon + Intel) with Developer ID notarisation. Same code, same catalog, same licence. Shipping alongside the auto-updater as one wave.
R·02b · in build Event clustering. Break the timeline into the events a human would remember — the trip, the birthday, the site visit — using time, place, camera and burst structure. Every event gets its own row in the browser.
R·03 · next On-device face grouping. Face embeddings computed on your CPU/GPU, clustering the same person across a decade of photos. Nothing leaves the machine. Off by default, one toggle to enable, one to erase the whole face index.
R·03 · next Semantic search & RAW dev. "beach at sunset in italy" runs against embeddings computed locally. Non-destructive RAW edits stored in XMP sidecars. Upscale and denoise powered by on-device models.

What SnapIT does not do.

No cloud storage.

We do not run a photo cloud. That is the exact model we exist to refuse.

No telemetry.

No analytics. No crash reports. No "usage reporting" toggle. Every network request the app makes is listed in the docs, and none of them are about you.

No training.

Your library is never used to train any model — ours or anyone else's. Read the Permanence Guarantee.
