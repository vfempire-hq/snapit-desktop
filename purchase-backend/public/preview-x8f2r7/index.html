Plug your socials in.

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube — every platform strips the story out of the photo before it delivers it. SnapIT reads the archive, not the render. Connect an account or drop a takeout ZIP and the original date, caption, likes, tags and place come back with the photo — filed alongside your camera and phone in the same library.

Two paths per platform — both durable.

Connect account (OAuth). Polished, keeps syncing. Works today on YouTube (Google), Instagram + Facebook (Meta Graph API), LinkedIn. Weaker on TikTok — no public consumer API — needs their Login Kit, app-review gated. Snapchat has no consumer API at all.

Import your data export. Every platform is legally required to hand you a takeout under GDPR — request it, wait 48 hours, download, point SnapIT at the ZIP. Works on every platform, cannot be cut off, restores every field the render strips. This is what ships first.

  • Instagram — Settings → Accounts Centre → Your information → Download your information
  • TikTok — Settings → Account → Download your data
  • Facebook — Settings → Download Your Information
  • YouTube — takeout.google.com
  • Snapchat — accounts.snapchat.com → Manage My Data
  • Threads / X / LinkedIn — same pattern in their respective settings

Legal by design — you already have this content.

Every extension click uses your own logged-in browser session. That means SnapIT sees exactly what you see — same as right-clicking Save-As, same as screen-recording. There's no scraping through our servers, no bypassing paywalls, no accessing content you can't view. If the platform can render it to you, SnapIT can file it for you.

For content that is paywalled or geo-locked, the extension refuses to save it and shows you why. We don't want to be the tool that gets your Netflix cancelled.
